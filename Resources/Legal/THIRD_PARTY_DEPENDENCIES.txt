ModelDial preview dependency inventory
======================================

This inventory records the components observed in the arm64 macOS 13
candidate bundle and the exact build inputs used to produce it.  It is
intentionally kept next to the complete license texts.  The release SBOM
generator (`build-support/generate-sbom.py`) re-checks the paths and hashes
against the candidate bundle; it does not treat this file as proof by itself.

Other bundled assets
--------------------

Sparkle 2.9.4, the LiteLLM pricing snapshot, and provider logo SVGs remain
documented in `Resources/Legal/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` and the accompanying
license files.  Their source and license claims are not duplicated here.

Verification boundary
---------------------

The Python installer metadata names XZ 5.2.3, Zstandard 1.5.7, libmpdec 4.0.0,
and OpenSSL 3.0.18.  If a future installer or bundle reports another version,
the validator must fail until this inventory and the license evidence are
reviewed.  No system libraries are represented as redistributable components.
